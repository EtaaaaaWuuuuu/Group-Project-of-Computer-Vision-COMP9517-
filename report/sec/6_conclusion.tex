\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We conducted a systematic empirical study of fine-grained 1{,}000-species classification on iNaturalist-2021 under data-constrained supervision (40 images/class). Our results demonstrate the necessity of ImageNet transfer learning, with modern CNNs (ConvNeXt V2 at $81.00\%$ Top-1) vastly outperforming handcrafted baselines ($5.14\%$). Grad-CAM visual interpretability confirms models focus on diagnostic anatomical traits rather than background context, while corruption benchmarks reveal pronounced vulnerabilities to motion and defocus blurs despite resilience to digital compression. These findings provide actionable insights for deploying robust ecological vision systems and highlight blur-resilient augmentation as a key future direction.
